Community page: how should the map show producers and meet-ups?

Issue: #12 Community page: map and meetup posters · 2026-10-24

Three ways to show where producers are and where meet-ups have happened. All three stay coarse: whole states or regions, never a pin on a town. The producer numbers are made-up sample data. The meet-ups are the real ones from the Meet-Ups page. The meet-up posters are a separate question, in their own lab.

What we need from you

Which map should we build: A, B or C? Any change you'd make to it? Comment your pick on issue #12.

We also need to know:

  • How would producers opt in? For example: a yes/no question when they sign up or text in, a reply keyword to the show's text line, or a short form on the site. We'd count only people who said yes.
  • An area code shows where a number was first issued, which may not be where someone lives now. Is a state from the area code close enough, or should producers tell us their state?
